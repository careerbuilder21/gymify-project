About GYMIFY

Learn about our gym management system and the team behind it.

What is GYMIFY?

GYMIFY is a web-based Gym Management System designed to automate daily gym operations. It replaces traditional manual methods with a modern, efficient digital platform. The system provides separate dashboards for gym owners (admins), trainers, and members — each with features suited to their role.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify gym management by providing an all-in-one system that handles member registration, attendance tracking, payment management, workout courses, and an integrated store for gym products like energy drinks and supplements. We aim to save time, reduce errors, and improve communication between gym staff and members.

Key Features

GYMIFY offers role-based access for Admin, Trainer, and Member. The Admin can manage the entire system, including adding trainers and members, tracking payments and attendance, managing workout courses and products, and generating reports. Trainers can view assigned members, mark attendance, create workout plans, and upload content. Members can view their attendance history, payment records, access workout courses, and purchase gym products from the store.

Project Background

This system was developed as a Final Year Project for the Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (2022-2026) at Govt. MAO Graduate College, Lahore, under the supervision of Prof. Irfan Shabbir.
